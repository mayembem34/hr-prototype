# Training assessments

Run `php artisan migrate` after deployment. Staff with assessment view and manage permissions can create one multiple-choice assessment for a course, add four-option questions, set a pass percentage and publish it. Published questions and the pass mark are locked to preserve existing results. Employees with active organization membership may attempt a published assessment for their own approved or directly assigned training. Answers are graded on the server; employees can retry failed attempts. The correct options are visible only to users with the assessment-view permission. Attempt summaries are visible to the employee and users with a separately scoped results-view permission.

A passing attempt is required before a published-assessment course can be marked complete, including by staff. Completing an assessment does not automatically complete training; use the progress action. Training already completed before publication remains completed. Assessment submissions are not proctored and do not verify that the employee watched a video. Editing a published assessment, question banks, timed exams and per-question response review are not supported.
