# Daily HR operations

Run `php artisan migrate` after deployment. Employees with active organization membership and a linked profile can clock in and out. Entries use UTC and can span midnight. A single entry may be open at a time. Users with scoped attendance view permissions can review entries; correction permission allows changes with a reason, and a separate history permission exposes audit events. Changes reject overlapping entries. Self-service attendance and notifications use session permissions plus active organization membership; attendance management permissions support own, unit, nested-unit and organization scopes.

Leave requests continue to count weekdays excluding configured holidays, and follow the supervisor then ordered officers. Daily in-system notifications alert the supervisor on submission, the next officer when their turn starts, and the employee when a decision is recorded. Notifications are not email or push messages; lifecycle notifications remain in their existing inbox. Attendance is a basic clock-in/out record; authorized managers can add a completed missed entry with a reason. Shift rules, break and overtime calculations, payroll calculation, location and biometric verification are not included. Existing leave routes are not automatically reassigned if an approver becomes inactive.
